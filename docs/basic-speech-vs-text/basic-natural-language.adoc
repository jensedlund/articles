== Natural language

=== Definitions of natural language

On 11th February 2023, the Wikipedia entry for "natural language" contained the following 
introduction:

----
In neuropsychology, linguistics, and philosophy of language, a natural language 
or ordinary language is any language that has evolved naturally in humans through 
use and repetition without conscious planning or premeditation. Natural languages 
can take different forms, such as speech or signing. They are distinguished from 
constructed and formal languages such as those used to program computers or to 
study logic.
----

The passage is relatively robust and has not been edited to any signifcant extent
